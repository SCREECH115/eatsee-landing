2. What EatSee is

EatSee is a mobile app for keeping a food diary and planning meals. The service consists of:

  • a food diary - you add a meal from a photo, from a description given by voice or text, by scanning a barcode or by hand, and artificial intelligence estimates its nutritional values;
  • a meal planner - suggested meals for a chosen period, with recipes and a shopping list, put together around the goals saved in your profile;
  • supporting features - water, weight and step logging, optional sync with your phone's health app, statistics and reports, streaks and badges, reminders and a referral programme.

Which features are available on a free account and with EatSee Premium is shown in the app. The app is under continuous development - individual features may be added, changed or withdrawn, but only for the reasons and on the terms described in sections 5 and 14.

9. Nature of results, meal plans and health disclaimer

The nutritional values calculated by EatSee are estimates. The AI recognises the dish and its size from a photo, a recording or a description, so results may differ from reality, sometimes considerably. They should not be treated as a measurement. AI-generated meal illustrations are for illustration only and do not show your meal.

Meal plans and recipes are suggestions. The nutritional values shown in them are estimates and may differ from your goals, and the actual values depend on the products you buy, their amounts and how you prepare them. Recipes are produced with the help of artificial intelligence and may contain mistakes, including mistakes that affect whether a meal is safe to prepare - read the whole recipe before you cook and use your own judgement.

Allergies and exclusions. The planner takes into account the diet, allergies and exclusions you provide, and leaves out recipes with excluded ingredients. This works only within the information you provided, and only for the meals the planner itself suggests - it does not cover meals you add to the plan yourself. The planner does not guarantee that a meal is free of an allergen - products may contain allergens, or traces of them, that are not visible in the ingredient name. If you have an allergy or intolerance, always read product labels.

The app is informational and motivational only - it is not medical or dietary advice, and meal plans are not a therapeutic diet and do not replace consulting a doctor or a dietitian. If you have a medical condition or follow a diet prescribed by a doctor, consult the plan with a specialist. It is not a medical device.
