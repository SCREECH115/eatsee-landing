2. What EatSee is

EatSee is a mobile app for keeping a food diary and planning meals.

  • Meal analysis: artificial intelligence recognises the dish in a photo of a meal or a product label, or in a description given by voice or text, estimates its nutritional values and saves them to your diary. You can also add a meal by scanning a barcode, from your favourites, by repeating it from previous days or by entering it by hand. For a meal added by voice or description without a photo, the app may generate an illustrative image.
  • Meal planner (Plan tab): after a short setup that uses the goals saved in your profile, the app builds a meal plan for a day or a week around your calorie and protein goal - with recipes, portions and preparation steps. You can swap a planned meal for one of the suggestions, log it to your diary with "I ate it", save it to your favourites and, when you drift from the plan, adjust the rest of the day. The plan produces a shopping list where you can tick off products and mark the ones you already have at home. With Premium, a plan can also prepare itself: every day or once a week, at the hour you choose, so that it is waiting for you in the Plan tab (off by default).
  • Other features: water, weight and step logging, sync with Apple Health and Health Connect, statistics and reports, streaks and badges, reminders and a referral programme.

9. Nature of results, meal plans and health disclaimer

The nutritional values calculated by EatSee are estimates. The AI recognises the dish and its size from a photo, a recording or a description, so results may differ from reality. AI-generated meal illustrations are for illustration only and do not show your meal.

Meal plans and recipes are suggestions. The AI picks ingredients from the EatSee catalog and their amounts and writes the recipe, while calories and macronutrients are calculated by EatSee's software from the catalog values - not by the AI. We save a new plan only when the planned meals of each day are within roughly ±5% of your calorie goal and ±10% of your protein goal. After a meal swap, "Adjust the rest of the day" or adding a meal from your favourites to the plan, values may differ more from the goal. Actual values depend on the products you buy, their amounts and how you prepare them. Some meals in a plan may come from ready-made EatSee recipe templates, and recipes written by the AI may contain mistakes - read the recipe before you cook.

Allergies and exclusions. The planner takes into account the diet, allergies, exclusions and equipment you provide, and automatically rejects recipes with excluded ingredients. This only works within the information you provided, and meals added to the plan from your favourites are not checked for it. The planner does not guarantee that a meal is free of an allergen - products may contain allergens, or traces of them, that are not visible in the ingredient name. If you have an allergy or intolerance, always read product labels.

The app is informational and motivational only - it is not medical or dietary advice, and meal plans are not a therapeutic diet and do not replace consulting a doctor or a dietitian. If you have a medical condition or follow a diet prescribed by a doctor, consult the plan with a specialist. It is not a medical device.
