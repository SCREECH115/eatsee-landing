EatSee Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

1. Data controller

The controller of your personal data is ASTROWARE Arkadiusz Woźniak, Rudka Kozłowiecka 102, 21-025 Niemce, Poland. For anything to do with your personal data, write to kontakt@eatsee.pl.

2. Data we process

  • Account data: e-mail address, name, account identifier, sign-in method (e-mail and password, Google or Apple), and whether your e-mail address is confirmed.
  • Profile data (health-related data): sex, date of birth, height, weight and weight history, activity level, weight goal, and the daily targets calculated from them (calories, macronutrients, water, steps).
  • Diary (health-related data): the meals you log (dish name, estimated nutritional values, the dish rating, date, time and portion), photos of meals and nutrition labels, meal illustrations generated by AI (an illustration is not a photo of your meal), favourite meals, water intake, and your streak, badges and achievements.
  • Descriptions and voice recordings: text you type when adding or correcting a meal, the voice recording when you log a meal by voice, and the text recognised from it. We do not store recordings on our servers, and we do not keep descriptions or recognised text in your diary - they are used only to produce the result (section 4).
  • Meal planner (health-related data): your food preferences - diet type, allergies, excluded foods, the products you mark as already at home, your cooking conditions and meal times - as well as saved plans with recipes and your shopping list, the recipe ratings derived from your choices in the planner and the recipes you have asked not to receive. Information about allergies and diet can reveal information about your health, so we treat it as health-related data.
  • Shared recipe database: when you log a meal from your plan to your diary, its recipe (name, ingredients, preparation steps) may be added to EatSee's shared recipe database and suggested to other users. They cannot see who the recipe came from.
  • Automatic planning (Premium, off by default): the settings of this feature (mode and time), the date you last opened the planner, device time zone and language, and a push notification token needed to tell you that your plan is ready. We store the token only after you turn the feature on, and remove it when you sign out or delete your account.
  • Apple Health and Health Connect (optional, after you turn on sync in the app and grant permission on your phone): EatSee writes the calories and macronutrients of your meals and your weight to them, and reads weight and step count from them. The step count itself stays on your phone - we do not send it to our servers. We do store the fact that you unlocked a step badge and pass that event to our usage statistics. We may suggest updating your profile with the weight from these apps - we save it only after you confirm.
  • Barcodes: the scanned barcode number. If you photograph the label of a product that is not in the database yet, its name and the nutritional values read from it go to EatSee's shared product database, which all signed-in users use. The entry contains the technical identifier of the account that added it, without a name or e-mail address.
  • Subscription, codes and referrals: plan status (free or Premium), the source of the entitlement (purchase, promo code or referral) and its end date, transaction identifiers provided by the app store, your referral code, who referred whom, and redeemed codes. To prevent abuse we also store a hash of your e-mail address with them. We do not process payment card data.
  • Technical data and usage statistics: counters for features that have usage limits, in-app events linked to your account identifier (for example saving a meal or a purchase) and, for products from the shared database, the scanned barcode number, device, system and app version information, crash and performance reports, server logs, and your device's IP address together with the approximate location (country, region) derived from it. Planner setup events contain the diet type and the number of selected allergies, without their names. If you try a photo analysis before creating an account, we store an anonymous identifier and the fact that the trial was used.
  • Support: the content of your message and the e-mail address you write from, and when you write from the app also a file attached automatically with the app and system version and the app's latest error messages.
  • Marketing consent and newsletter: whether you consented to receiving marketing messages, when you gave that consent and which version of the clause you accepted, and when we send a message, the e-mail address it was sent to, the date and the delivery status.
  • Data on your device: the app keeps its settings (including reminders and appearance), language, the name used in reminders and temporary files (for example a voice recording or a PDF report) in your phone's storage.

Providing your data is voluntary, but some of it is necessary to use the service: without an e-mail address you cannot create an account, without profile data we cannot calculate your daily targets, and without food preferences the planner cannot build a plan. If you do not provide it, the corresponding features will not work.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • providing the service - running your account, the meal diary and AI analyses, the meal planner with its shopping list, reminders, sync with health apps, the referral programme, the e-mails needed to run your account and handling your enquiries - Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract);
  • processing health-related data - your profile, your meal diary (including meal photos, nutritional values and your weight), and the diet, allergies and exclusions in the planner - in order to calculate your daily targets, keep your diary and build meal plans - Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR (explicit consent). You give that consent by knowingly entering this data in the app, and you can withdraw it at any time: by deleting the data in your planner preferences, by writing to kontakt@eatsee.pl, or by deleting your account. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it; after you withdraw consent, the features that rely on this data stop working;
  • usage statistics and crash and performance reports, to improve the app and fix failures - Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in maintaining and improving the app);
  • running the shared product database (barcodes) and the shared recipe database, to make results and plans more accurate for all users - Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest);
  • handling subscriptions and preventing abuse (limits, promo codes, referrals) - Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in protecting against abuse);
  • sending marketing messages to your e-mail address - Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent, section 5);
  • tax and accounting obligations - Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR (legal obligation).

4. AI features

To recognise a meal and estimate its nutritional values, to draw an illustration of a dish and to build a meal plan, we send data to an artificial-intelligence provider that processes it outside the EEA (sections 6 and 7). We send only what is needed to produce the result:

  • the photo of a meal or a label (including a trial analysis before you create an account), the description you type, and the voice recording together with the text recognised from it;
  • in the meal planner - your food preferences, including diet type, allergies and exclusions, and the daily targets the plan is to be built around;
  • the app language.

We do not send your name, e-mail address or account identifier. Under our agreement with that provider, data sent this way is not used to train its models. With automatic planning the data is sent at the time you set, without you opening the app.

We do not take decisions about you based solely on automated processing that would produce legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affect you (Art. 22 GDPR). Analysis results, illustrations and meal plans are estimates or indicative only, matched to the targets and preferences you provide, and you can always correct or reject them.

5. Marketing e-mails

If you give separate consent, we will send news, tips, recipes and promo codes granting free premium days to your e-mail address. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent), and the sending itself follows the Polish Act on the Provision of Electronic Services and electronic communications law. We send these messages through an external e-mail delivery provider (section 6).

  • Consent is entirely voluntary - withholding it does not limit your use of the app or your account in any way.
  • You can withdraw it at any time in the app (Profile → EatSee newsletter) or by writing to kontakt@eatsee.pl. If your e-mail app shows an unsubscribe button, using it sends us that request by e-mail. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of messages sent beforehand.
  • Only people aged 16 and over can consent to marketing communication. If you are under 16, do not tick this consent.
  • We use your e-mail address for this purpose until you withdraw consent or delete your account. The record of messages sent (e-mail address, date and delivery status) is not removed together with your account - see section 8.

6. Data recipients

We do not sell your data. We entrust its processing to providers that act on our instructions and only to the extent needed to run the service. They are:

  • a cloud infrastructure provider (US) - authentication, including Google sign-in, database, photo storage, server functions and server logs;
  • an artificial-intelligence provider (US) - analysis of photos, descriptions and recordings, speech-to-text, meal illustrations and meal plan generation (section 4);
  • a subscription management provider (US) - subscription status and granting Premium from promo codes and referrals;
  • product analytics providers (EU and US) - app usage statistics; these services derive an approximate location (country, region) from the IP address;
  • an app crash and performance reporting provider (EU) - crash reports together with the device's IP address;
  • an e-mail delivery provider (US) - address confirmation, password reset, the welcome code and the newsletter;
  • a mobile services and notification provider (US) - delivering "plan ready" notifications through the Apple and Google notification systems, and delivering app updates; when the app checks for an update it sends this provider device and app version information and its IP address;
  • a web hosting provider (EU) - the server that runs the eatsee.pl website.

We have a data processing agreement with each of these providers which requires them to protect your data to a standard no lower than the one described in this policy and forbids them from using it for their own purposes.

Data is also received by:

  • Apple and Google - as independent controllers, in connection with payments in the App Store and Google Play and with Sign in with Apple or Google;
  • Google - Google Ads cookies on the eatsee.pl website, as described in section 12;
  • an open, public food product database - when you scan a barcode, your device may ask such a database about the product directly, and it receives the barcode number and your device's IP address;
  • other EatSee users - products you add to the shared barcode database and recipes added to the shared recipe database (section 2).

We give categories of recipients rather than the trade names of individual companies. If you want to know the current list of providers by name, write to kontakt@eatsee.pl and we will send it to you.

7. Transfers outside the EEA

Some of your data is processed outside the European Economic Area, in the United States. The following are held or processed in the US:

  • the database and the files of your account - your profile data, your meal diary with its photos and your meal plans - and the server functions of our cloud infrastructure provider;
  • the data we send to the artificial-intelligence provider (section 4);
  • the data processed by the subscription management provider, the e-mail delivery provider and the mobile services and notification provider;
  • one of the product analytics services.

We only use providers outside the EEA where we can base the transfer on one of two grounds: on a European Commission implementing decision finding an adequate level of protection (the EU-US Data Privacy Framework), where the provider is covered by it, or - where it is not - on Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR). Write to kontakt@eatsee.pl and we will tell you which ground applies to a given category of recipients and give you a copy of the safeguards.

Crash and performance reports, the second product analytics service and the eatsee.pl website are processed on servers within the European Economic Area. The analytics and crash reporting services also receive your device's IP address, from which they derive the approximate location.

8. Retention and deletion

  • account, profile, diary and planner data - until you delete your account; you can change or remove allergies and exclusions at any time in the planner preferences;
  • data needed for billing - for the period required by Polish tax and accounting law (as a rule 5 years, counted from the end of the year in which the tax payment fell due);
  • consents and the records proving them - until you withdraw consent, and then for the limitation period for claims;
  • records that protect against abuse - for as long as that purpose requires, no longer than 24 months from their last use, including after you delete your account;
  • usage statistics - no longer than 24 months; crash and performance reports - no longer than 12 months; server logs - no longer than 90 days.

You can delete your account directly in the app (Profile → Account → Delete account). This permanently removes your login account, profile, meal diary together with its photos and illustrations, favourites, water and weight entries, meal plans, planner preferences (including allergies), automatic planning settings together with the notification token, your private recipes, usage counters and the Premium status stored by EatSee.

After deletion the following remain:

  • recipes from your plans that were previously added to the shared recipe database - no longer linked to your account;
  • products you added to the shared barcode database, with the technical identifier of the account that added them;
  • records that protect against abuse: redeemed codes and daily e-mail sending limits, with an account identifier or a hash of the e-mail address;
  • the newsletter send record with your e-mail address, date and delivery status;
  • the anonymous identifier and the record of a trial analysis used before creating an account;
  • data held by the providers listed in section 6, and your purchase history in the app store - for the periods those parties apply;
  • data EatSee wrote to Apple Health or Health Connect - delete it in those apps;
  • settings and temporary files on your phone - remove them by uninstalling the app.

The shared databases, the records that protect against abuse and the newsletter send record are not removed automatically together with your account. You can ask us to remove them by writing to kontakt@eatsee.pl. Remember, too, that deleting your account does not cancel a subscription in the App Store or Google Play - do that in the store settings.

9. Your rights

You have the right to: access your data and obtain a copy of it, rectify it, erase it, restrict processing, data portability, object to processing based on our legitimate interest (including usage statistics), and withdraw consent at any time - without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

Send any request by e-mail to kontakt@eatsee.pl. The app currently has no feature for downloading a copy of all your data, so we will prepare and send that copy on request. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority - in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl).

10. Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not direct the service to younger children and do not knowingly collect their data. If we learn that a younger child has created an account, we will delete it along with the data. A separate age rule applies to marketing consent - see section 5.

11. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and access is restricted by permission rules - each user can only access their own data. The exceptions are the shared databases described in section 2: all signed-in users can read the shared product database (barcodes), and recipes from the shared recipe database may appear in other users' plans.

12. Cookies and device storage

The eatsee.pl website uses Google cookies (Google Ads) solely to measure ad performance and site traffic. These cookies are set only after you consent, which you can grant or decline in the banner on your first visit - your choice is remembered in your browser. You can change or withdraw it at any time with the cookie settings link (Ustawienia cookies) in the eatsee.pl footer, or by clearing the site data. Without consent we rely only on cookieless, aggregate measurement. The website also stores your chosen theme (light or dark) in your browser. The e-mail confirmation and password reset page loads a script from our authentication provider.

The EatSee mobile app does not use cookies. It does, however, store settings and the identifiers needed for usage statistics and crash reports on your phone. The app does not collect your device's advertising identifier.

13. Changes to this policy

We will announce material changes to this policy in the app or on this page. The current version is always available at this address.
