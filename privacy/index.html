1. Data controller

2. Data we process

  • Account data: e-mail address, name, account identifier, sign-in method (e-mail and password, Google or Apple), whether your e-mail address is confirmed, and encrypted credentials.
  • Profile data (health-related data): sex, date of birth, height, weight, activity level, weight goal, the calculated calorie and macronutrient targets, and your water and step goals.
  • Diary: meals (dish name, estimated nutritional values, health score, date and time, portion, link to a meal plan), photos of meals and nutrition labels, meal illustrations generated by AI, favourite meals, water intake, weight history, streak, badges and achievements.
  • Descriptions and voice recordings: text you type when adding or correcting a meal, the voice recording when you log a meal by voice, and the text recognised from it. We do not store recordings on EatSee servers, and we do not store descriptions or recognised text in your diary. We send them to OpenAI for the purposes described in section 4.
  • Meal planner: number and times of meals, diet type, allergies (chosen from the list of 14 EU allergens and typed in your own words), foods you dislike or do not want to eat (including ones you type in), preparation time, kitchen equipment, breakfast style, priorities, how often dishes may repeat and items marked "I have it", as well as saved meal plans with recipes, swap suggestions, shopping list state, recipe scores based on your choices and recipes you do not want to get. Information about allergies and diet can reveal information about your health, so we treat it as health-related data.
  • Shared recipe database: when you log a meal from your plan to your diary, its recipe (name, ingredients, preparation steps) may be added to EatSee's shared recipe database and suggested in the plans of other users who use the app in the same language. Other users cannot see who the recipe came from.
  • Automatic planning (Premium, off by default): the chosen mode (daily or weekly), hour and weekday, device time zone and language, when you last opened the planner, and a push notification token (Expo) needed to tell you that your plan is ready. We store the token only after you turn the feature on, and remove it when you sign out or delete your account.
  • Apple Health and Health Connect (optional, after you turn on sync in the app and grant permission on your phone): EatSee writes the calories and macronutrients of your meals and your weight to them, and reads weight and step count from them. The step count itself stays on your phone - we do not send it to EatSee servers. We do store the fact that you unlocked a step badge (for example 100,000 steps) and send that event to our usage statistics (PostHog, Google Analytics for Firebase). We may suggest updating your profile with the weight from these apps - we save it only after you confirm.
  • Barcodes: the scanned barcode number. If a product is not in the database and you photograph its label, the product name and the nutritional values read from it go to EatSee's shared product database, which all signed-in users use. The entry contains the technical identifier of the account that added it, without a name or e-mail address.
  • Subscription, codes and referrals: plan status (free or Premium), the source of the entitlement (purchase, promo code or referral) and its end date, transaction identifiers provided by the app store and RevenueCat, your referral code, who referred whom, and redeemed promo codes and the welcome code. To prevent abuse, for codes and referrals we also store a hash of your e-mail address. We do not process payment card data.
  • Technical data and usage statistics: daily feature counters (AI analyses, refinements, voice entries, illustrations, plans and swaps), in-app events linked to your account identifier (for example saving a meal, adding water, viewing the Premium screen, a purchase, and for products from the shared database also the barcode number), device, system and app version information, crash and performance reports, server logs, and your device's IP address together with the approximate location (country, region) derived from it by our usage statistics (PostHog, Google Analytics for Firebase) and crash reports (Sentry). Planner setup events contain the number of meals, the diet type and the number of selected allergies and equipment items, without the names of the allergies. If you try a photo analysis before creating an account, we store an anonymous identifier and the fact that the trial was used.
  • Support: when you write to us from the app, a file with the app and system version and the app's latest error messages is attached to the message. We also receive the content of your message and the e-mail address you write from.
  • Marketing consent and newsletter: whether you consented to receiving marketing messages, when you gave that consent and which version of the clause you accepted. When we send a newsletter, we record the e-mail address it was sent to, the date and the delivery status.
  • Data on your device: the app keeps its settings (including reminders and appearance), language, the name used in reminders and temporary files (for example a voice recording or a PDF report) in your phone's storage. Reminders are scheduled and prepared by the app on your phone.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • providing the service (account, diary, AI analyses, voice logging, meal illustrations, the meal planner with its shopping list, automatic planning, reminders, sync, the referral programme, e-mails needed to run your account, and support) - Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract);
  • processing health-related data (profile, nutritional values of meals, weight, allergies and diet provided in the planner) - Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR (explicit consent, given by voluntarily providing this data during profile and planner setup; you can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account, and you can also remove allergies and exclusions, or change your diet, at any time in the planner preferences);
  • usage statistics and crash reports (PostHog, Google Analytics for Firebase, Sentry), to improve the app and fix errors - Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest);
  • running the shared product database (barcodes) and the shared recipe database, to make results and plans more accurate for all users - Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest);
  • payment handling and abuse prevention (limits, promo codes, referrals) - Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR (legitimate interest);
  • sending marketing messages to your e-mail address - Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent);
  • tax and accounting obligations - Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.

4. AI features

AI features run on the EatSee server, which sends data to OpenAI. The app never connects to OpenAI directly. We do not send your name, e-mail address or account identifier to OpenAI. We send:

  • for meal analysis from a photo or a label: the photo (including a trial analysis before you create an account), the app language and a hint if you add one, and for a correction of the result - its content together with the previous result;
  • for meals logged by voice or description: the voice recording, which OpenAI turns into text, and then the description you confirm, from which the AI estimates the nutritional values;
  • for meal illustrations: when you add a meal by voice or description without a photo, the dish name and your description are used to generate an illustrative image with AI. The illustration is saved with the meal - it is not a photo of your meal;
  • in the meal planner: diet type, allergies and exclusions (including ones typed in your own words), disliked foods, equipment, the preparation time limit, breakfast style, priorities, how often dishes may repeat, items marked "I have it", calorie and protein targets for each meal, names of your favourite dishes and of the dishes you logged most often in the last 4 weeks, and for a meal swap - the name of the dish being swapped and the reason (if you choose one), as well as the language. We do not send your sex, age or weight. With automatic planning this data is sent at the time you set, without you opening the app.

In meal plans, the AI picks ingredients from the EatSee catalog and their amounts and writes the recipe, but it does not provide calories or macronutrients - EatSee's software calculates them from the ingredient catalog. Before a new plan is saved, the software checks that it respects your diet, allergies, exclusions, equipment and preparation time limit, and that the planned meals of each day are within roughly ±5% of your calorie goal and ±10% of your protein goal. Some meals may come from ready-made EatSee recipe templates.

According to the OpenAI API policy, data submitted through the API is not used to train models. Analysis results, illustrations and meal plans are estimates or illustrations for informational purposes only - they do not constitute medical or dietary advice.

5. Marketing e-mails

If you give separate consent, we will send news, tips, recipes and promo codes granting free premium days to your e-mail address. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent), and the sending itself follows the Polish Act on the Provision of Electronic Services and electronic communications law. We send these messages through Resend.

  • Consent is entirely voluntary - withholding it does not limit your use of the app or your account in any way.
  • You can withdraw it at any time in the app (Profile → EatSee newsletter) or by writing to kontakt@eatsee.pl. If your e-mail app shows an unsubscribe button, using it sends us that request by e-mail. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of messages sent beforehand.
  • To demonstrate consent we store its status, the date it was given and the version of the clause you accepted.
  • Only people aged 16 and over can consent to marketing communication. If you are under 16, do not tick this consent.
  • We use your e-mail address for this purpose until you withdraw consent or delete your account. The record of messages sent (e-mail address, date and delivery status) is not removed together with your account - see section 8.

6. Data recipients

We use the following processors, which process data on our behalf:

  • Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC - Firebase infrastructure (authentication, including Google sign-in, database, photo storage, server functions and server logs) and Google Analytics for Firebase (usage statistics, including the approximate location derived from the IP address);
  • OpenAI, L.L.C. - analysis of photos, descriptions and recordings, speech-to-text, meal illustrations and meal plan generation (section 4);
  • RevenueCat, Inc. - subscription status management and granting Premium from promo codes and referrals;
  • PostHog - app usage statistics; the service derives an approximate location (country, region) from the IP address;
  • Sentry - app crash and performance reports, together with the device's IP address;
  • Resend - sending e-mails: address confirmation, password reset, the welcome code and the newsletter;
  • Expo - delivering "plan ready" notifications (through Apple and Google notification services) and app updates;
  • Hetzner - the server that runs the eatsee.pl website.

Data is also received by:

  • Apple Inc. / Google LLC - payment processing in the App Store and Google Play, and Sign in with Apple (as independent controllers);
  • Google - Google Ads cookies on the eatsee.pl website, as described in section 12;
  • Open Food Facts - when you scan the barcode of a product that is not in EatSee's shared database, the app asks the open Open Food Facts database about it directly, which receives the barcode number and your device's IP address;
  • other EatSee users - products you add to the shared barcode database and recipes added to the shared recipe database (section 2).

7. Transfers outside the EEA

Some providers (including OpenAI, RevenueCat, Resend and Expo, and depending on the service also Google) may process data in the United States. Transfers take place on the basis of an adequacy decision (EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission. The app sends usage statistics (PostHog) and crash reports (Sentry) to the European servers of these services - together with the IP address from which those services derive the approximate location.

8. Retention and deletion

We store your data until you delete your account. This also applies to meal plans and planner preferences - you can change or remove allergies and exclusions at any time in the planner preferences. You can delete your account directly in the app (Profile → Account → Delete account). This permanently removes your login account, profile, meal diary, meal photos and illustrations, favourites, water and weight entries, meal plans, planner preferences (including allergies), automatic planning settings together with the notification token, your private recipes, usage counters and the Premium status stored by EatSee.

After deletion the following remain:

  • recipes from your plans that were previously added to the shared recipe database - no longer linked to your account;
  • products you added to the shared barcode database, with the technical identifier of the account that added them;
  • records that protect against abuse: redeemed promo, referral and welcome codes and daily e-mail sending limits, with an account identifier or a hash of the e-mail address;
  • the newsletter send record with your e-mail address, date and delivery status;
  • the anonymous identifier and the record of a trial analysis used before creating an account;
  • data held by providers: purchase history at RevenueCat and in the app store, usage statistics (PostHog, Google Analytics for Firebase), crash reports (Sentry), e-mail delivery logs (Resend) and server logs (Google Cloud) - for the periods set by those providers; data sent to OpenAI is kept in line with OpenAI's terms;
  • data EatSee wrote to Apple Health or Health Connect - delete it in those apps;
  • settings and temporary files on your phone - remove them by uninstalling the app.

The shared databases, the records that protect against abuse and the newsletter send record are not currently removed automatically. You can ask us to remove them by writing to kontakt@eatsee.pl. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription in the App Store or Google Play - do that in the store settings. Data required for billing purposes is retained for the period required by law.

9. Your rights

You have the right to: access your data, rectify it, erase it, restrict processing, data portability, object to processing and withdraw consent at any time (without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing). The app has no feature for downloading a copy of all your data - you can request such a copy, object to usage statistics or make any other request by e-mail. You may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority (in Poland: the President of the Personal Data Protection Office, uodo.gov.pl). For any data-related matters contact us at: kontakt@eatsee.pl.

10. Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not direct the service to younger children and do not knowingly collect their data. Consent to receiving marketing messages, however, can only be given by people aged 16 and over, as required by Polish data protection law. If you are under 16, do not tick this consent.

11. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and access is restricted by security rules - each user can only access their own data. The exception is the shared product database (barcodes), which all signed-in users can read. The shared recipe database is available only to the EatSee server, which uses it when building plans.

12. Cookies and device storage

The eatsee.pl website uses Google cookies (Google Ads) solely to measure ad performance and site traffic. These cookies are set only after you consent, which you can grant or decline in the banner on your first visit - your choice is remembered in your browser. You can change or withdraw it at any time with the Ustawienia cookies link in the eatsee.pl footer, or by clearing the site data. Without consent we rely only on cookieless, aggregate measurement (Google Consent Mode). The website also stores your chosen theme (light or dark) in your browser. The e-mail confirmation and password reset page (auth.eatsee.pl) loads the Firebase library from Google servers.

The EatSee mobile app does not use cookies. It does, however, store settings and the identifiers needed for usage statistics (PostHog, Google Analytics for Firebase) and crash reports (Sentry) on your phone. The app does not collect your device's advertising identifier.
